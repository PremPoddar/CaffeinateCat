Why CaffeinateCat

Small app. Big peace of mind.

Everything you need to keep your Mac running, nothing you don't.

☕

Caffeinate

Keeps your screen on and prevents idle sleep with a single click, or a right-click on the cup. On the moment you launch, if you like.

💻

Lid-closed mode

Shut the lid, unplug, and walk away — your Mac keeps running on battery. Perfect for travel.

⏱️

Built-in timers

Run indefinitely or switch off after 15m, 30m, 1h or any time up to 23h 59m. Timers keep counting while the Mac sleeps, and show when they end.

🔋

Battery-aware

The cup is a battery gauge, and keep-awake switches itself off at 20% and again at 10%, so a Mac in a bag can't run itself flat.

🔄

Updates itself

New versions arrive through Sparkle, signed and verified before they install. A handful of Swift files, no Dock icon, no background bloat.

🛡️

Safe by design

Restores normal sleep when you turn it off or quit, and a tiny watchdog does it even after a crash or Force Quit. It can't leave your Mac stuck awake.

It lives in your menu bar

Click the cup and the panel drops down; right-click it to switch keep-awake on or off in one go. The drink inside shows your battery, and the cup steams in lid-close mode.

  • One switch per mode — turning one on turns the other off
  • Pick 15m, 30m, 1h or a custom time, or leave it running indefinitely
  • The countdown shows in the menu bar and the panel, with the time it ends
  • Options: let the display sleep, cut off on low battery, start at login
  • Check for Updates… right in the panel

Under the hood

How it works

01

Idle assertions

Caffeinate uses macOS's own beginActivity API to hold off idle and display sleep. No permissions required.

02

The lid-close trick

For lid-closed mode it sets the SleepDisabled flag via pmset — the only reliable way to stay awake with the lid shut on battery.

03

Ask once, run silently

A tiny, tightly-scoped permission rule is installed with one native admin prompt (Touch ID works too). After that, it just works.

04

Never stuck awake

A watchdog in its own process restores sleep if the app dies, and the app re-checks every 30 seconds and after each wake.
